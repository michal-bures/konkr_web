## Privacy policy

<div style="padding:16px; background-color: lightgoldenrodyellow;margin: 0px;margin-bottom:16px; border-left: 4px solid #ffc107">
<b>The short version:</b>
<ul style="margin-bottom: 0; margin-top: 16px">
 <li>
  If you give me your <b>email address</b> (on the feedback form or when you sign in), I keep it safe. That is:
  <ul>   
    <li> I will <b>only</b> use it for the purpose for which it was provided. </li>
    <li> I will <b>not</b> use it for any marketing or advertising purposes without your express consent.</li>
    <li> I will <b>not</b> share or sell it to any third party that could use it for marketing purposes.</li>
  </ul>
 </li>
 <li>Other than the email, I don't collect or store any other personally identifiable information. </li>
 <li>I do collect <b>usage statistics</b> and <b>crash logs</b> when you play the game. These don't include your name or IP address. You can turn the usage statistics off in the game's settings, under <b>Privacy</b>.</li>
</ul>
</div>


### 1. What is collected

1.1 The **email address** you provide when you sign in or enter it in the in-game feedback form. 
 
1.2 **Usage statistics** and **Crash logs**, which may include:
    
  * information about your actions in the game (levels played, turns, settings, features used)
  * statistics about your gameplay (total playtime, levels completed, etc.)
  * your country, worked out from your time zone (your IP address is never read or kept)
  * your browser's time zone, OS, window size, frame rate, storage space and other technical information
  * a random id the game makes for your browser, or your account's id if you sign in, so I can count players
  * they do **not** contain your name or IP address. The email you add to a feedback message stays with that message (see 2.1)
 
### 2. Why is it collected

2.1 **Email address** is collected for the following purposes:
 
 * When you provide your email address while signing-up or when you sign-up through a third party provider (such as Google or Facebook),
     your email address is used in the sign-in process and stored at the user database.
 * When you provide your email address in the in-game feedback form, I may contact you with to answer your question 
   or ask for additional information regarding your feedback. Your message and its email reach me in a private Discord channel, which keeps them,
   and my statistics server, which deletes the email after 12 months and keeps the message without it.
 * If you subscribe to news emails in the game, I send you news about Konkr a few times a year (see News emails below).
 * Your email address will not be used for any other purposes than the ones listed above.

2.2 **Anonymized usage metrics** are collected, because they offer valuable insight into the way real users interact
     with the game. This in turns allows me to improve the user experience, balance game difficulty and in general make more informed
     design decisions when improving the game.

2.3 **Crash logs** are sent automatically when an unexpected error is encountered and you see a red error notification popup. 
     This helps me detect, reproduce, analyze and hopefully fix bugs in the game.

### 3. Disclosure to third parties
3.1 When you sign-in, your **email address** is processed and stored by [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) (owned by Google LLC) as the authentication provider.
   Your email is processed according to [terms of use of the Google Cloud Platform](https://cloud.google.com/terms/), section 5.2. This includes:
     *Google will only access or use Customer Data to provide the Services and TSS to Customer or as otherwise instructed by Customer and will not use it for any other Google products, services, or advertising.*
     For more context see [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). 
 
3.2 **Usage statistics** go to my own statistics server: Google Cloud in Belgium receives them, and my own computer keeps them.
 My server never reads or keeps your IP address, and the only email in them is one you add to a feedback message.
   
3.3 **Crash logs** are processed by Sentry.io (usage governed by [Sentry privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)).
 These do **not** contain emails, IP addresses or any other personally identifiable information.

3.4 **Feedback** you send in the game goes to a private channel on [Discord](https://discord.com/privacy), where I read it, including the email if you add one.

### Usage statistics

You can turn the usage statistics off in the game: open the settings and switch **Privacy → Usage statistics** to OFF. The game then sends what it was still waiting to send, with a note that you turned them off, and nothing after that. The choice holds for this browser, for everyone who plays in it.

### News emails

If you're signed in, you can turn on **news emails** in the game, and I'll email you when a new expedition or other major update comes out, at most 4 times a year. They're only for players 16 or older. I send them only because you agreed to it, and you can withdraw that at any time.

**What I keep:** that you said yes or no, when, and the sentence you agreed to. It's stored with your progress in Firebase (Google), and the emails go to the address of the account you signed in with.

**Unsubscribing:** turn it off in **Profile → News emails** in the game, or delete your account, which deletes your answer with it.

### Deleting your data

**Your account:** if you signed in on [konkr.io](https://www.konkr.io), open your **Profile** in the game, choose **Delete data...** and then **Delete account...**. This deletes your account, including your sign-in email, and the copy of your progress stored with it. The game may ask you to sign in again first. You can also email me at [mike@konkr.io](mailto:{{site.email}}) and I'll do it for you.

**Kongregate:** if you play on Kongregate, your progress is stored under your Kongregate player, without an email. Email me at [mike@konkr.io](mailto:{{site.email}}) to have it removed.

**Your browser:** **Delete data...** also deletes your progress, islands, favorites or game history from your browser. To remove everything the game stores in your browser, clear the site data for konkr.io in your browser's settings.
